FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which sports does MatchPoint support?

MatchPoint 1.0 supports tennis and padel.

Can I use MatchPoint without an Apple Watch?

Yes. The iPhone app works on its own.

Does it work with a Samsung Galaxy Watch?

No. The current watch app is built for watchOS and requires Apple Watch.

Which languages are available?

Dutch, English, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese.

Does MatchPoint upload my match history?

No MatchPoint server is used for your match history in version 1.0. Match and player data is stored locally.

Why does MatchPoint ask for Contacts access?

Only if you choose to select a player from Contacts. The chosen information is used locally.

How do I report a problem?

Email matchpointscoreapp@gmail.com.
