Privacy information for MatchPoint 1.0.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

1. Overview

MatchPoint is designed to work without a MatchPoint account and without a MatchPoint cloud service. The developer does not collect, sell or share personal data from the MatchPoint app with third parties for advertising or tracking purposes.

2. Data stored on your device

Players, matches, scores, statistics, settings, planned matches and match history are stored locally on your device for the app to function.

3. Apple Watch

If you use MatchPoint with Apple Watch, active match information is exchanged between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch so scoring and match state can stay synchronized.

4. Contacts

If you choose to select a player from Contacts, MatchPoint may request access. This is used only to let you choose contact information for a player. MatchPoint does not upload your address book to a MatchPoint server.

5. Notifications

If you enable match reminders, MatchPoint can schedule local notifications on your iPhone. These reminders are created on your device.

6. Analytics, advertising and tracking

MatchPoint 1.0 does not include advertising, third-party advertising trackers or a MatchPoint analytics service.

7. Children

MatchPoint is a sports scorekeeping utility and does not intentionally collect personal information from children through a MatchPoint server.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated if MatchPoint features or data practices change. The current version will remain available on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact matchpointscoreapp@gmail.com.
